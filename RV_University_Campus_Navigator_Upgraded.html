    Navigation Help

    How to find a room and understand the directions.

    1. Choose your floorSelect Ground, First, Second or Third Floor. The indoor plan changes to that floor.
    2. Set where you areChoose the entrance, stairs or a room from the starting-point list. GPS is for outdoor location and may not work accurately indoors.
    3. Search your classroomType a room name or number, such as Harvard Classroom or G-01, then choose the result.
    4. Follow the blue routeRead each numbered instruction. The blue line is the suggested path on the current schematic plan.
    5. Use Google Maps outsideTap Open campus in Google Maps for outdoor travel to the university. Indoor classroom directions are shown separately.
    6. Report missing roomsIf a classroom is not listed or its position is wrong, add the verified room location to the campus directory and update the floor plan.
    Important: Phone GPS usually cannot identify the exact classroom or floor. Accurate indoor turn-by-turn navigation needs a verified floor plan and indoor positioning infrastructure (such as mapped access points or beacons). This project currently provides a schematic route demo, not a live indoor positioning guarantee.

    About

    Campus Navigator is a Web Technology project for RV University, Mysore Campus. It helps students and visitors find rooms and facilities and reach them with clear directions.

    The indoor room layout currently contains draft schematic data. Exact room positions, floor connections and routes must be verified with the university before operational use. Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
